Feasibility Analysis

Before you invest in design and permits, find out what your property and budget can realistically support, so you move forward with eyes open.

Discuss this service
  • Site and zoning constraints review
  • Scope versus budget reality check
  • Early identification of permit and code hurdles
  • Go / no-go recommendations in plain language
Primary deliverableA clear feasibility summary with risks, options, and next steps.

Discuss this service
  • Cross-discipline drawing review

  • Requests for information and discrepancy logs
  • Trade plan walkthroughs and technical briefings

Permit Processing

Keep your project moving through applications, plan checks, agency follow-ups, and revisions. We coordinate the permit process and bring the right professionals into the conversation.

Discuss this service
  • Pre-submission review of application requirements
  • Building, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, and grading application coordination
  • Liaison with planners, plan checkers, and inspectors
  • Tracking correction sheets and coordinating revised submissions

Discuss this service
  • Itemized lumber, concrete, steel, finishes, and utility quantities
  • Labor allowances with stated assumptions
  • Subcontractor bid comparisons and variance review
  • Material alternatives and value engineering coordination
