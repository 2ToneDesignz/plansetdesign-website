A disciplined four-phase approach keeps the client, design professionals, and field team aligned from concept to closeout.

Discovery & Technical Audit

Start with the site, the scope, and your vision.

Key deliverableProject feasibility assessment
  • Establish project goals, priorities, and investment limits.
  • Review available surveys, site information, and zoning constraints.
  • Define feasibility questions and the professional team needed.

Virtual Pre-Construction

Make key decisions before work begins.

Key deliverableCoordinated models and estimate
  • Develop agreed BIM models and architectural visualizations.
  • Complete quantity takeoffs and identify drawing conflicts.
  • Review alternatives and confirm the working budget.

Permitting & Procurement

Organize approvals and compare the right scopes.

Key deliverablePermit packages and scoped proposals
  • Coordinate municipal applications, follow-ups, and revisions.
  • Prepare subcontractor scope packages and compare bids.
  • Review vendor proposals and scheduling requirements.

Execution & Advocacy

Stay informed through the final walkthrough.

Key deliverableProgress records and closeout package
  • Record site progress and review milestone observations.
  • Track change orders, budget variances, and inspection corrections.
  • Coordinate punch-list walkthroughs and closeout records.
